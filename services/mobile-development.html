Questions, answered.

iOS, Android or both?

Usually both. With cross-platform frameworks like Flutter or React Native we build one codebase for both stores, saving time and budget. We go fully native when performance demands it.

Do you handle App Store submission?

Yes. We prepare store listings, screenshots and privacy details, and manage the review process with both Apple and Google.

What happens after launch?

Apps need care: OS updates, new devices and user feedback. Our maintenance plans keep your app healthy and improving.

Can the app connect to our existing systems?

Yes. We regularly connect apps to existing databases, ERPs, CRMs and payment gateways through secure APIs.
