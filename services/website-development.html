Questions, answered.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We set up an easy-to-use CMS and give your team a walkthrough, so you can edit text, images and pages without touching code.

Do you provide hosting and a domain?

We can register your domain and host your site on fast, secure infrastructure — or deploy to your existing hosting if you prefer.

Can the website be bilingual?

Of course. We build English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese sites with proper language switching and SEO for each version.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. We review what’s working, migrate your content and protect your search rankings with proper redirects.
