Questions, answered.

Why custom instead of off-the-shelf?

Off-the-shelf tools are great until your process doesn’t fit. Custom software matches how your team actually works, scales with you and avoids per-user licence creep.

Can you work with our existing system?

Yes. We often extend or integrate with existing systems rather than replace them, protecting the investment you’ve already made.

How do you handle data migration?

Carefully: data mapping, test runs and validation before switchover, so nothing gets lost along the way.

Who owns the system?

You do. Once paid in full, you own the source code and your data, with documentation for future development.
