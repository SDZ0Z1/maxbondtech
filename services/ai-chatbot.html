Questions, answered.

Will the chatbot make things up?

We ground the bot in your own approved content and set clear rules, so it answers from your information, says so when it doesn’t know — and hands over to your team.

Can it really reply in Cantonese?

Yes. It can reply in English, Cantonese-style written Chinese and Mandarin, in Traditional or Simplified characters, matching each customer automatically.

Is our data safe?

We design every chatbot with Hong Kong’s Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance in mind, limit what the bot can access, and never use your data to train public models.

Which channels can it work on?

Your website, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram — and internal tools like Slack or Microsoft Teams.
