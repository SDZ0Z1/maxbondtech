Hi there,
here are the questions we hear most often. Short answers, no jargon.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is different, so we quote based on your scope. After a free consultation, you’ll receive a clear, fixed-price proposal — no hidden fees, no surprise invoices.

How long will it take?

A typical company website takes 4–8 weeks. AI chatbots usually take 2–4 weeks, while mobile apps and custom systems take 2–6 months depending on features. We’ll give you a realistic timeline up front.

Will I own the website, app or source code?

Yes. Once the project is paid in full, the code, designs and content are yours. No lock-in, ever.

Can you work with our existing systems?

Absolutely. We regularly integrate with, extend or modernise existing websites and systems instead of starting from scratch.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes a free support period, and we offer monthly maintenance plans for hosting, security updates, backups and improvements.

Can your AI chatbots speak Cantonese?

Yes — English, Cantonese-style written Chinese and Mandarin, in Traditional or Simplified characters. The bot replies in whichever language your customer uses.

Do you work with clients outside Hong Kong?

We do. We’re based in Hong Kong and work with clients across Asia and beyond, with meetings on Zoom, Teams or in person.
